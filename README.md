### Hey, I'm Mike.

I build things for people who need them, and sometimes for the ones who don't know they need them yet.

I spent about fifteen years working with veterans in jails, transitional housing, and permanent supportive housing, from helping folks find a place to live to acting as an SSA Rep Payee. Trauma-informed care, PTSD, addictions: that work shaped how I see systems and the people stuck inside them.

These days I'm a full-stack developer and snowboard instructor in upstate New York. I run **Spliced Infinities**, a small web design and hosting shop, and I build tools for problems I've seen firsthand. I'm not great at leaving things broken when I think I can help.

---

#### What I'm Working On

- **Ashes & Aether**: a post-apocalyptic multiplayer RPG set in real upstate New York, built on OpenStreetMap and USGS data. Unreal Engine 5 client, TypeScript server, Rust simulations for wildlife and climate. Folklore made real by a returning alien Aether, villages you build and share, guilds that hold ground in the wild. It's the MUD I always wanted, finally with a horizon.

- **[Shred Gremlins](https://shredgremlins.com)**: connects snowsports students, instructors, and mountains so lessons don't start from scratch every time. The biggest problem in snow sports education is continuity, and this is my answer to it.

- **[Veteran Connector](https://veteran-connector.org)**: a veteran support platform. Took me six years to finish the submissions portal. It's done now.

- **[All Faiths on Fire](https://allfaithsonfire.org)**: an interfaith community space. Functional, but still needs a lot of writing.

- **[EF-Vita](https://ef-vita.net)**: multi-tenant managed hosting for Eve Frontier players who want to run a tribe without dealing with code, databases, or infrastructure. Easy and cheap, on purpose.
